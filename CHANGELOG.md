# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Passive mode. The skill writes first from the repository, marks the gaps and leaves its questions in `00-questions.md` / `00-perguntas.md`, which the developer answers when they can. No interview: a question in the chat only when a whole document is blocked (two cases, rule 12). Later runs update only what changed; a document edited by hand is never overwritten. `references/pack-files.md` gives the format of every file of a pack and the contract of the end-of-turn hook (Claude Code `Stop` event) that the plugin will ship.
- Writing rules adapted from ASD-STE100 (Simplified Technical English, Issue 9) to French and Portuguese, as rules 21 to 30 of `references/writing-style.md`, with a table of approved words aligned with the templates. The skill claims no conformance to the standard and no approval by ASD.
- `style_check.py` reads sentences and paragraphs across lines, enforces rule 21 (20 words per sentence in documents 3 and 4, 25 elsewhere) and rule 22 (three sentences per paragraph, list item or table cell; six for a list item or table cell in documents 3 and 4), counts a URL, an e-mail address or a `{{field}}` as one word, and ends no sentence after an abbreviation such as "M." or "Sr.", or before a closing quote. `--max-words N` and `--max-sentences N` replace the limits.
- `coverage_check.py` accepts a draft document 4 with fewer than three symptoms when it points to the pack's questions file, and says so with a warning; it fails when the line names a questions file that is not in the pack.
- `inventory.py` skips the pack's own files by their names, wherever they are, and every `--skip` folder. `style_check.py` and `coverage_check.py` skip the questions file and the proposals written beside documents edited by hand; `secret_scanner.py` reads them.
- Templates: sentences shortened to the new limits; the restore warning now comes before the step; "programme" / "programa" for the program on the machine; plain words for the backup in documents 1 and 6; rule 5 of document 5 back to three sentences; "O que mais ninguém sabe consertar" and "Le plus souvent" gone, because they claimed what no source says; the decision rights of the owner and the person to warn are fields filled only from an answer (`owner_decides`, `notify_who`); calques out of the Portuguese templates ("redatar", "em claro", "gesto").
- Rules from the test rounds. An answer says what it says: no widening, no dropped hedge, no "I do not know" turned into "does not exist"; what a question supposed is not an answer. The marker goes in the sentence of each of the four fields of rule 3, including the open points of document 6. A symptom of document 4 takes its check and its "fixed when" only from the recorded cause and fix, and the symptom that stops everybody is never the one dropped for length. A step or command that the open points say cannot work is written with the gap named. Every document of a pack gives the first safe gesture where no procedure exists.
- Questions file in plain words, for the developer: an opening that says the last block is not for him, a block for the owner or the technical referent with who answers, numbers that never repeat (new questions from 11), one thing asked at a time, and "Respondidas" that keep his words word for word. A hedged answer fills no field of rule 3.
- Updating. `coverage_check.py --merged` checks the pack as it will be once the user has merged the proposals; `style_check.py` reads proposals; when one language gets a proposal the other gets one too; "Checks" in `verification.md` is replaced whole and its numbers come from the script. Tested: a run with nothing changed touches nothing; a repository change plus a hand edit keeps the hand edit byte for byte and writes `proposta-03-rotina.md` and `proposition-03-routine.md`.
- Rule 31 (the skill's own): documents 1, 4 and 6 hold no technical term; `style_check.py` flags a list of them in French and Portuguese. `secret_scanner.py` no longer takes a lowercase setting such as `charset=utf-8` for a password. `coverage_check.py` matches service names without regard to case. `inventory.py` creates the pack folder. The scripts say "give an absolute path" when a folder is missing.
- Example and fixture regenerated: `examples/estante/` and `tests/fixtures/estante-pack/` come from a full run in passive mode (first run, the developer answers, hook run), checked by four independent reviewers and corrected by hand on 26 lines. The round 3 pack is gone. 20 unit tests.
- Known, not fixed: document 6 gives the owner no action of her own; the 400-word limit of documents 1, 4 and 6 and the fixed lines of the templates pull against each other, mostly in French; the Portuguese of the example uses "cópia" where the approved word is "backup"; the Stop hook script is not written yet (the plugin, version 0.2).

## Bastão 0.1.0 (2026-10-30)

First version that can be tried.

- Six documents in French and Brazilian Portuguese, from the repository and ten questions, with up to three follow-up questions.
- Four check scripts (standard library, no network): inventory, secret scanner, coverage check with word counts and FR/PT parity, style check.
- A fictional repository, a full simulated run and unit tests, so the skill can be tested without real data.
- A complete example pack in `examples/estante/`.

Tested on a real system (two rounds, private) and on the fictional repository (two rounds, public).
